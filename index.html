Net Worth Calculator

Net Worth Calculator is a free personal-finance web app that helps you track everything you own and owe and see your net worth in ₹. Add your assets and liabilities, split them across household members, and watch your net worth change over time with charts and monthly history. It is private and offline-first: your data stays on your device, or — only if you choose to sign in with Google — in a single spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. There is no backend server, no analytics, and no ads; nothing is ever sent to the developer. The same app is also available for Android on Google Play.
